Why TabSyncBridge wins for mixed-browser setups

  • The only Safari ↔ Chromium bridge Neither iCloud Tabs nor Chrome Sync can show Safari tabs in Chrome or Chrome tabs in Safari. TabSyncBridge is the only tool that bridges both ecosystems.
  • Close tabs remotely See a tab open on your other device? Send a close request directly to the browser that owns it. Neither iCloud Tabs nor Chrome Sync offer this.
  • Private by architecture Not "privacy-friendly" — architecturally private. Your tab data physically cannot reach an external server because the sync only works on your private local network.
