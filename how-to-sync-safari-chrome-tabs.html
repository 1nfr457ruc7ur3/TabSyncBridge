TabSyncBridge

How to sync Safari and Chrome tabs on the same Wi-Fi.

See your iPhone Safari tabs in Chrome, Edge, or Brave — and your desktop tabs on your iPhone — without signing into a cloud account. Everything stays on your own private Wi-Fi network.

Start 3-month free trial Add Chrome extension

Setup in 5 minutes

  1. Install the iPhone app Download TabSyncBridge from the App Store on your iPhone. Open it and start the free 3-month trial — no payment details required upfront.
  2. Add the browser extension Install the free TabSyncBridge companion from the Chrome Web Store or Microsoft Edge Add-ons. Works with Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, and Vivaldi.
  3. Install the desktop bridge The bridge connects the browser extension to your local network. Follow the desktop bridge setup guide — takes about 60 seconds.
  4. Connect on the same Wi-Fi Open the TabSyncBridge app on your iPhone while your desktop is on the same Wi-Fi. Your devices discover each other automatically via Bonjour/mDNS — no manual IP configuration needed.
  5. View and manage your tabs Your Safari and Chrome tabs now appear together. View tab titles from any linked device, and send close-tab requests to the browser profile that owns the tab.

What you get

  • Cross-browser tab list See Safari, Chrome, Edge, and Brave tabs in one view. No need to switch between browser ecosystems.
  • Remote close Close a tab on your desktop from your iPhone, or vice versa. The request goes directly to the browser that owns the tab.
  • Zero cloud accounts No Google sign-in, no iCloud, no developer relay server. Tab data never leaves your local network.

Who is this for?

TabSyncBridge is designed for people who use Safari on iPhone and a Chromium browser on their Mac, PC, or Linux machine. If you want to see all your tabs in one place without creating another cloud sync account — or without sending your browsing data through Google or Apple servers — this is the tool for you.

Common use cases include developers working across Safari and Chrome, privacy-conscious users who avoid cloud sync, households with mixed Apple and Windows devices, and anyone who finds iCloud Tabs unreliable with non-Safari browsers.

Frequently asked questions

  • Does it work without internet? Yes. TabSyncBridge only needs your local Wi-Fi. It works on airplane mode with Wi-Fi enabled, on networks without internet access, and on private subnets.
  • Is it really private? Yes. No analytics SDKs, no tracking pixels, no third-party relay. Tab data stays on your own network between your own devices.
  • What does the trial include? Full access for 3 months. After that, it is £2.99/month through Apple. Cancel anytime in your iPhone Settings → Subscriptions.
